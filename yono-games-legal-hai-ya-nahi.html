Kya Yono Games Legal Hain? India 2026 ka Pura Sach

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Yono Games Sach team
Short jawab: Nahi. 1 May 2026 se Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (PROGA) poori tarah laagu hai. Paise lagakar khele jaane wale online games — chahe skill ke hon ya luck ke — India mein band hain. Yono naam se chal rahe rummy, slots, spin aur bet apps isi category mein aate hain.

PROGA kaanoon kya kehta hai?

Parliament ne ye kaanoon August 2025 mein paas kiya, aur iske Rules 1 May 2026 se laagu ho gaye. Kaanoon online games ko teen hisson mein baant-ta hai:

CategoryStatusUdaharan
Online money games (paisa jama karke jeetne ki ummeed)BandRummy, teen patti, slots, poker, fantasy, colour prediction, "Yono" type apps
Online social games (bina daav ke)AllowedNormal mobile games jinmein paisa jeetne ka system nahi
E-sports (registered)Allowed / promotedBGMI jaise competitive games, recognised tournaments

Kis par saza hai?

Kaanoon teen kaamon ko apradh banata hai: money game offer karna (app chalana), uska advertisement karna (website, YouTube, Telegram par promotion), aur uske liye payment process karna. Ye apradh cognisable aur non-bailable hain — police bina warrant karyawahi kar sakti hai. Operators ke liye 3 saal tak jail aur ₹1 crore tak jurmane ka praavdhaan hai.

Agar aap kisi Yono/rummy app ka referral link, "download" button ya bonus code share karte ho, to aap "advertisement" wale hisse mein aa sakte ho — sirf app chalane wala hi nahi.

Kya sirf khelne wale (player) ko saza hai?

Central kaanoon mein mukhya nishaana operators, advertisers aur payment karne wali companies hain. Lekin kuch rajyon ke apne kaanoon bhi hain — jaise Tamil Nadu, Telangana aur Andhra Pradesh ne apne star par online money games par rok lagayi hai. Sabse badi baat: gair-kaanooni app aapka paisa rok le to aapko koi regulator nahi bachayega. Aapka paisa pura risk par hai.

Fir bhi ye apps chal kaise rahe hain?

Zyadatar Yono-type apps Play Store par nahi hote. Ye APK file, Telegram channel aur "all yono app list" jaisi websites se baante jaate hain, aksar videsh se chalte hain, aur payment ke liye baar-baar naye UPI ID badalte hain. Sarkar hazaaron betting/gambling URLs block kar chuki hai, isliye ye apps naye naam se baar-baar aate rehte hain.

Agar main pehle se khel raha hoon to?

Turant naya paisa daalna band karo. Jo balance bacha hai uska screenshot, transaction ID aur UPI reference save karo. Agar withdrawal ruk gaya hai to hamari paisa phansa complaint guide dekho.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Kya Yono games India mein legal hain 2026 mein?

Nahi. PROGA 2025 ke Rules 1 May 2026 se laagu hain, aur paise lagakar khele jaane wale sabhi online games band hain. Yono naam ke rummy, slots aur spin apps isi mein aate hain.

Kya Yono game khelne par jail ho sakti hai?

Central kaanoon ka mukhya nishaana app chalane wale, promotion karne wale aur payment process karne wale hain. Lekin kuch rajyon (jaise Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh) ke apne kaanoon hain, aur gair-kaanooni app mein paisa doobne par aapko koi kaanooni suraksha nahi milti.

Kya Yono games ka SBI YONO app se koi rishta hai?

Nahi. YONO State Bank of India ke banking app ka naam hai. 'Yono 777', 'Yono Rummy' jaise games ka SBI se koi sambandh nahi; ye naam bas bharosa dikhane ke liye use hota hai.

Kaun se online games abhi bhi legal hain?

Bina paise ke daav wale social/mobile games aur registered e-sports legal hain. Jahan aap paisa jama karke zyada paisa jeetne ki ummeed rakhte ho, wo band hai.
