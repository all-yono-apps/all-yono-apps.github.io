Yono Game Mein Paisa Phans Gaya? Step-by-Step Complaint Guide

Last updated: 15 November 2026 · Yono Games Sach team
Short jawab: Turant 1930 (National Cyber Crime Helpline) par call karo ya cybercrime.gov.in par complaint darj karo. Jitni jaldi report, utna zyada mauka ki paisa aage jaane se pehle rok diya jaaye. App ko aur paisa mat bhejo.

Step 1: Saboot jama karo (aaj hi)

Ye cheezein ek folder mein save karo: deposit ke UPI/bank transaction ID (UTR), jis UPI ID par paisa gaya uska screenshot, app ke wallet balance aur withdrawal request ka screenshot, app ka naam aur download link, aur support se hui chat. App delete karne se pehle screenshots le lo.

Step 2: 1930 par call / cybercrime.gov.in

1930 National Cyber Crime Helpline hai. Fraud ke turant baad call karne par bank ko paisa freeze karne ko kaha ja sakta hai. Online complaint ke liye cybercrime.gov.in par jaakar "Report Other Cyber Crime" chuno aur financial fraud ki details bharo. Complaint number sambhal kar rakho.

Step 3: Apne bank ko batao

Apne bank ke customer care ya branch mein likhit complaint do, cyber complaint number ke saath. UPI ke maamle mein apne UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) mein bhi "dispute / report" karo.

Step 4: Consumer forum (registered company ho to)

Agar app kisi India ki registered company ka tha aur usne bina kaaran paisa roka, to consumer commission mein online complaint e-Daakhil portal se kar sakte ho. Videsh se chalne wale ya bina naam-pate wale apps par ye raasta kaam nahi karta — wahan cyber complaint hi sahi hai.

"Recovery agent" se bacho. Paisa doobne ke baad aksar log Telegram/WhatsApp par aate hain jo "fee lekar paisa wapas dilane" ka daawa karte hain. Ye doosra scam hai. Sirf 1930, cybercrime.gov.in, police ya bank se baat karo.

Bade, purane apps (ban se pehle wale) ka balance

Kaanoon aane ke baad bade platforms ne cash games band kiye. Final Rules mein wallet balance lautaane ka koi alag niyam nahi rakha gaya, jis par experts ne chinta jatayi hai. Agar kisi bade platform mein aapka purana balance phansa hai, to pehle us company ke grievance officer ko email karo (likhit jawab maango), phir zaroorat ho to consumer forum jao.

Aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal (FAQ)

Yono game mein paisa phans gaya, kahan complaint karein?

National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 par call karo, ya cybercrime.gov.in par 'Report Other Cyber Crime' mein complaint darj karo. Saath mein apne bank aur UPI app mein bhi report karo.

Kya complaint se paisa wapas mil sakta hai?

Guarantee nahi hai, lekin turant report karne par bank paisa freeze kar sakta hai. Jitni der karoge, paisa aage transfer hone ka khatra utna badhega.

Withdrawal pending hai aur app aur deposit maang raha hai?

Aur paisa bilkul mat bhejo. Ye scam ka pattern hai. Saboot save karke 1930 par report karo.

Kya koi agent fee lekar paisa wapas dila sakta hai?

Nahi. 'Recovery' ke naam par fee maangne wale khud scammer hote hain. Sirf sarkari channels ka upyog karo.
